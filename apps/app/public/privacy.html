Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Shelf Thread does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites. Your saved books, lists and settings stay on your device unless you choose to connect an outside book service.

This policy covers the Shelf Thread app for iOS and Android and the website at shelfthread.com, operated by Benjamin Bradley ("we", "us"). Shelf Thread helps you search for books, explore how books, authors and subjects connect, and check whether your library has a copy or it is available via other sites.

Information we collect

Stored only on your device. Saved books, works and lists, recent searches and viewing history, search filters, theme and display preferences, and the libraries you add (a name and catalog link). We never receive this data unless you export it yourself.

Search and browsing requests. To look up books, the app sends your search terms, ISBNs and the IDs of books, authors or subjects you open to our server. Like most web servers, ours records each request with its time, the address requested and your IP address. We use these logs to keep the service running, stop abuse and count overall usage.

Camera. If you scan a barcode, the app uses your camera only to read the ISBN. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or uploaded. Camera access is optional; you can type an ISBN instead.

Connected accounts (optional).

  • Hardcover. If you connect Hardcover, you sign in on Hardcover's own website. We receive and store your Hardcover user ID, username and access tokens so the app can read and update your Hardcover shelves for you. We never see your Hardcover password.
  • Open Library. If you connect Open Library, you paste in your Open Library API access and secret keys. We store those keys, your username and a session cookie so the app can read and edit your Open Library lists. We never see your Open Library password.

Feedback. If you send feedback, it goes through a Google Form. Only what you choose to type is collected, under Google's privacy policy.

Website analytics. The website (not the app) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or identify individual visitors.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, health data, payment details or advertising identifiers. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run Shelf Thread:

  • Answer your searches and show book, author, subject and library details
  • Sync shelves and lists with Hardcover or Open Library when you ask the app to
  • Limit request rates and block abuse
  • Count overall usage, such as popular searches and daily visitors, to decide what to improve

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

Storage, retention and security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. Session identifiers are stored on our server only as one-way hashes. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we limit what we keep to what the app needs.

Your choices

  • Export your data. Use Backup in Settings to save a copy of your saved books, lists and settings.
  • Delete your data. Use Delete all data in Settings to erase everything stored on your device and disconnect any connected accounts. You can also remove items one at a time, or uninstall the app.
  • Disconnect accounts. Disconnecting Hardcover or Open Library in Settings deletes the stored tokens or keys from our server right away. You can also revoke access from your Hardcover or Open Library account settings.
  • Camera. Turn camera access off at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Server logs. To ask us to delete log entries tied to your IP address, contact us below.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us to use them; we will respond within 30 days.

Children, tracking, changes and contact

Children. Shelf Thread is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

No tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not use Apple's advertising identifier.

Changes. If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at shelfthread.com/privacy.html. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact. Questions or requests: support@shelfthread.com.

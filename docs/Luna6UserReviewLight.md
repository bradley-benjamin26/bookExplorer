# Luna6 User Review — Light Theme

## Scope

The participants could not be shown a rendered live screen: browser access was unavailable, and the expected local server ports were not responding from the review session. Their feedback below is based on the app's light-theme source styles, not on live visual inspection.

The light palette uses warm cream paper (`#FAF7F0`), dark ink (`#1F2430`), a deep green action color (`#2B5951`), softly colored metadata chips, Fraunces headings, and Inter body text.

## Participant feedback

- **Age 13:** Called it warm and cozy; liked the green scan button and friendly chips. Thought the chip colors might feel random next to green, and saved rows might need more definition.
- **Age 18:** Found the palette calm and bookish, but worried the pastel chips could be too subtle for quickly browsing comics and sci-fi. Suggested stronger chip colors or borders.
- **Age 22:** Thought the cream background and font pairing sounded inviting and readable. Suggested clearer separation between the many sections on book pages.
- **Age 35:** Liked the clear scan button and soft metadata colors. Felt pale row and card borders might blend into the background; suggested darkening them.
- **Age 68:** Found the palette welcoming and the main action easy to spot. Raised the strongest concern about small text: suggested increasing 13px helper text and chip labels to at least 14px, and making clickable chip outlines clearer.
- **Age 29:** Liked the warm, bookish feel and distinct metadata colors. Suggested making the export/import area more visually distinct on the long My Library page and watching for small, muted text.

## Shared reaction

The cream-and-green palette and font pairing seem well suited to book browsing, and the scan action has a strong visual focal point. The recurring styling concerns are small muted text, subtle borders, and softly colored chips that may not read clearly as interactive.

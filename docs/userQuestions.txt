The following are questions to be asked to the users outlined in personas.txt. Subagents should be deployed
to review the application and answer the questions following the persona characteristics. If the agent makes an assumption
that is outside of the persona document, please list the assumption. Testers could potential go straight to shelfthread.com if that is easier for testing.

1) What are your initial impressions when you view the application. Which do you like better, the mobile
view or the desktop view. Why?
2) Search for a book that might interest you. Were you able to find one? Go to its page. Describe your experience. Is the information presented helpful? Is some unhelpful?
2a) Navigate between work and an edition, does the distinction make sense?
2b) Try to add terms and notes to a book page.
3) Click on the graph view button. Click on a few nodes to explore the experience. What are your thoughts about the experience?
3a) Click on a subject term and view its page. Is the page helpful or not? Describe your experience.
4) Save some items to a list. View the recommendations based on the list. What are your thoughts? Did you find any good recommendations?
5) Search for an author and try to access its page. Where you able to find and access it? Describe your experience and thoughts.
Is the information helpful or unhelpful?
6) Compare the standard/light view with the dark view. What do you like or not like about each one. Which do you prefer?
7) What are your 3 favorite things about this app?
8) What are your 3 least favorite things about this app?
9) What new features would interest you in this app?
10) What are your thoughts about the application overall?
11) Do you find the app easy to use? Did you need to use any accessibility features?
How did that work out?
12) Would you use this app again?
